Features

Everything the rota needs. Nothing it does not.

Built for managers who schedule the same team every week and would rather not start from a blank grid each time.

01

Templates that repeat

Save a week you are happy with and reuse it. Shiftly carries over patterns and only asks about the gaps.

02

Open shift marketplace

Post a gap and the people qualified for it get notified. First to claim it gets it, with your approval if you want.

03

Swaps without the chat

Staff arrange cover between themselves inside the app. You see the request and approve in one tap.

04

Time off that fits

Requests land against the live rota, so you can see what breaks before you say yes.

05

Overtime warnings

Shiftly flags anyone heading over their contracted hours while you are still building the week.

06

Timesheets that export

Hours flow from the rota into a timesheet you can send straight to payroll. CSV or a direct export.

How it works

Three steps to your first rota.

Add your team

Import from a spreadsheet or type them in. Set contracted hours, roles, and who is qualified for what.

Build a week

Drag shifts onto the grid, or let Shiftly fill it from your usual pattern and adjust what it got wrong.

Publish

Everyone gets the rota on their phone. Changes after publishing notify only the people affected.

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Do staff need to install anything?+
There is an app, but it is optional. Staff can view the rota and claim shifts from a link in a text message if they would rather not install anything.
Can we import our existing rota?+
Yes. Upload a spreadsheet of people and shifts and Shiftly will map the columns. Most teams are set up inside an afternoon.
What happens when someone calls in sick?+
Mark the shift open and everyone qualified gets notified at once. In most teams it is claimed before the manager has finished their coffee.
Does it handle multiple sites?+
On the Business plan, yes. You can schedule across locations, share staff between them, and report on each separately.
How does billing work?+
You pay for people who are actually scheduled in a given month. Seasonal teams are not charged for staff sitting idle out of season.
